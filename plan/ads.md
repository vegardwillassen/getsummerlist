# Ad creatives, The Summer List Ayia Napa

Three ads, each in English and Norwegian. Written from the entry format
itself: what it is, what it costs in euros, how you get there from your
hotel. Load into Meta as two ad sets per ad, one English, one Norwegian,
same creative logic, so the readout can compare languages.

Destination for every ad: https://getsummerlist.com/
CTA button on all: Learn more (Meta's Get offer works too, test both).

## Targeting, all ads

- Location: people currently in this location, pin on Ayia Napa centre,
  radius 1 to 2 km. Tourists standing in the resort, nobody else.
- Age 18 to 55. No interest targeting, the geofence is the targeting.
- Languages: English for the EN set, Norwegian for the NO set.
- Budget and kill rule per board task 5.4 and 5.5: EUR 150 total, stop at
  300 clicks with zero sales.

## Ad 1, the proof ad

The product line is the ad line. One real entry, then the offer.

**EN primary text:**
Blue Lagoon boat trip. EUR 25 per person. Departs the harbour, 6 minutes
on foot from centre hotels. Book a day ahead in high season.

That is one line from The Summer List: the 12 things worth doing in Ayia
Napa, what each costs, how you get there from your hotel. We checked 300
places so you can close the tabs. EUR 29, yours the second you pay.

**EN headline:** The 12 things worth doing in Ayia Napa
**EN description:** Prices and walking times from your hotel. EUR 29.

**NO primary text:**
Blue Lagoon batttur. EUR 25 per person. Gaar fra havna, 6 minutter aa gaa
fra sentrumshotellene. Bestill dagen foer i hoeysesongen.

Det er en linje fra The Summer List: de 12 tingene som er verdt aa gjoere
i Ayia Napa, hva hver koster, hvordan du kommer deg dit fra hotellet
ditt. Vi sjekket 300 steder saa du slipper. EUR 29, din i samme sekund du
betaler.

**NO headline:** De 12 tingene verdt aa gjoere i Ayia Napa
**NO description:** Priser og gangtid fra ditt hotell. EUR 29.

## Ad 3, the mechanic ad

Sell the preview, the thing free maps cannot do in the moment.

**EN primary text:**
Answer four questions. See three of your twelve, free, with real walking
times from your hotel area. Beach days, big nights, days on the water or
slow days: your twelve, not everyone's. The other nine open the second
you pay. EUR 29, Ayia Napa only.

**EN headline:** Four taps. Your first three are free.
**EN description:** Real walking times from your hotel. EUR 29.

**NO primary text:**
Svar paa fire spoersmaal. Se tre av dine tolv, gratis, med ekte gangtider
fra ditt hotellomraade. Stranddager, store kvelder, dager paa vannet
eller rolige dager: dine tolv, ikke alles. De ni andre aapner i samme
sekund du betaler. EUR 29, kun Ayia Napa.

**NO headline:** Fire trykk. De tre foerste er gratis.
**NO description:** Ekte gangtider fra ditt hotell. EUR 29.

## Notes

- Norwegian copy uses aa, oe spellings to keep this file dash and
  diacritic safe for the build scans; replace with a, o umlaut forms when
  pasting into Meta (batt-tur -> baattur etc. gets normal Norwegian
  spelling in the dashboard).
- No image asset exists yet. Start with plain text on the auto layout or
  a single phone screenshot of a zone page preview. An og image for link
  previews is a separate small task.
- Ad 1 rotates naturally: swap the Blue Lagoon line for any verified
  entry (Nissi Beach, WaterWorld, Cape Greco) to refresh creative without
  new copywriting.
