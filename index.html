Questions

What people ask.

Do we need to download an app?

No. The list is a web page you add to your home screen. It opens full screen like an app, with no App Store, no account and no updates to install.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Everything is stored on your phone after the first open. Photos load when you have coverage; the rest works in flight mode.

How do we share it between two phones?

You each sign in with your name and a four-digit PIN. When you want to share wishes and tick-offs, one of you shows a QR code and the other scans it. Done in seconds, and nothing touches a cloud.

When and how do we pay?

Right after you send the order, a Pay now button appears (and we can email you the same link): card, Apple Pay or Google Pay, hosted by Stripe. Your list normally arrives within one hour of payment between 09:00 and 22:00 CET; orders paid at night arrive by morning. The price includes Norwegian VAT.

Our place isn't one of the three. Can you still do it?

Not this summer. Depth is the whole product, so we only sell lists for places we've researched properly. Pick “somewhere else” in the form and leave your destination in the notes; you get an email the day your place goes live, and no charge before that.

Can we be more than two?

The list is built for two phones with a wish list each. If you're a group of friends or a family, write it in the order and we'll work it out together.
